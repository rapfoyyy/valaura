# VALAUERA
### Grow Together, Naturally.

Valauera is a digital platform that brings together plant enthusiasts through community interaction, plant exploration, gardening education, and plant care experiences.

More than just a plant marketplace, Valauera aims to create a welcoming digital space where people can discover plants, exchange knowledge, share experiences, and grow together.

## About The Project

Valauera is designed for everyone, from beginners discovering the world of plants to experienced collectors and gardening enthusiasts.

The platform combines botanical exploration, community engagement, and personal plant care in one ecosystem.

## Core Features

### Plant Marketplace
Explore plants, seeds, and gardening essentials through product categories and discovery filters.

### Plant Community
Share plant stories, participate in discussions, ask questions, exchange knowledge, and connect with other enthusiasts.

### Plant Studio
A collection of plant care tools, including:
- Plant Finder
- Plant Clinic
- Care Planner
- Plant Journal

### Botanical Knowledge
Discover educational articles, gardening guides, and inspiration to deepen your understanding of plants.

### Personal Experience
Personal profiles, wishlists, community activities, language preferences, and customizable website appearance.

## Design Philosophy

Valauera follows a **Botanical Editorial Minimalism** approach, combining modern editorial aesthetics with natural visual elements.

Design principles:
- Forest green and ivory color palette.
- Botanical photography and editorial typography.
- Clean, intuitive navigation.
- Accessible and readable interfaces.
- Responsive desktop and mobile layouts.
- Indonesian, English, and Japanese language support.
- Light and dark themes.

## Vision

To create a calm, inclusive, and accessible digital environment where people can connect with nature through technology, knowledge, and community.

Valauera is not simply about discovering plants. It is about discovering inspiration, sharing knowledge, and growing together.
